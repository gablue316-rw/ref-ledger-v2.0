After confirmation: get started with Ref Ledger

Before adding your first game, sign in and set up the information you will use to track your officiating.

  1. Set up your associations

    Open Associations and add each organization you officiate for. Enter the association name and any other details requested, then save it.

    You will select an association when recording games, payments, and expenses.

  2. Add your officials

    Open Officials and add yourself and the officials you work with. Enter each official's name and available contact information, then save the record.

    These officials will be available when assigning the officiating crew to a game.

  3. Add your sites

    Open Sites and add the schools, parks, gyms, or other locations where you officiate. Save each site using a name you will recognize when entering a game.

  4. Select your sports

    Open Settings, find the sports selection, and select the sports you officiate, such as Basketball or Softball. Save your selections.

    Your selected sports will be available when adding or updating games.

  5. Set up your levels

    Open Levels and add the competition levels you officiate, such as Junior Varsity, Varsity, or College. Save each level so you can select it when entering games.

Ready to get started? After confirming your email, sign in and complete these setup steps, then open Games to add your first game. Use the Dashboard to review and manage your schedule.
